### R CAPSTONE PROJECT
Description: 🚚 Trucking Income Analyzer

An R Shiny-based application developed as an undergraduate capstone project to help streamline administrative processes for a trucking service business.

The system automates workers' wage calculations, computes total income per trip, analyzes monthly income performance, and provides digital recordkeeping through Excel integration.

🎯 Project Objectives

- Automate workers' pay calculations based on trip commissions
- Calculate total income per trip after expenses and workers' pay
- Analyze income performance against the monthly average
- Visualize income categories using bar plots
- Provide downloadable Excel reports for selected months
- Reduce manual bookkeeping and calculation errors

🛠️ Technologies Used

- R
- RStudio
- R Shiny
- Excel
- "readxl"
- "dplyr"
- "writexl"
- "ggplot2"

📊 Key Features

Wage Calculation

The system calculates crew and driver commissions based on the trucking business's existing compensation procedures.

Income Analysis

The application calculates total income per trip and categorizes results as Above Average or Below Average based on the selected month's average income.

Interactive Dashboard

Users can select a month and view trip details, worker salaries, total income, and an income visualization.

Excel Integration

Users can download the computed monthly results as an Excel file directly from the application.

🎓 Project Background

This project was developed as a group undergraduate capstone project for the Bachelor of Science in Statistics program at Rizal Technological University and was designed for use by E. A. Francisco Trucking.

The project aimed to support the transition from manual bookkeeping toward a more efficient computerized system for trucking service administration.

# R Programming Fundamentals: Numerical and Financial Analysis

## Overview

This project contains a collection of R programming exercises focused on numerical computation, vector operations, and practical financial analysis. The exercises demonstrate the use of R vectors, arithmetic operations, mathematical formulas, matrices, and functions such as **c()**, **cbind()**, **rbind()**, **seq()**, and **paste()**.

The project applies R programming to practical scenarios including profit calculation, daily savings analysis, monthly energy costs, loan payment calculations, temperature conversion, stock price changes, and other numerical problems.

## What the Assignment Covers

>>| # | Project/Exercise | What I Did in R |
>>|---|---|---|
>>| 1 | *Profit Calculation* | Calculated selling prices, profit per item, quantity sold, and total daily profit |
>>| 2 | *Daily Financial Analysis* | Calculated daily savings and savings percentages from income and expenses |
>>| 3 | *Monthly Energy Cost Calculation* | Calculated monthly electricity costs from energy consumption using a fixed ₱12/kWh rate |
>>| 4 | *Loan Payment Calculation* | Calculated monthly loan payments for ₱30,000–₱100,000 loans at 4% interest across 1–5 year terms |
>>| 5 | *Area of a Triangle* | Calculated the area of a triangle using a mathematical formula |
>>| 6 | *Age in Dog Years* | Converted human ages into dog years using a given formula |
>>| 7 | *Temperature Conversion* | Converted Celsius temperatures to Fahrenheit |
>>| 8 | *Stock Price Change* | Calculated daily stock price changes using closing price − opening price |
